Sequence

Five steps from brief to acceptance.

One partner carries the project across every stage, so nothing is lost between the drawing and the room.

  1. 1

    Brief and site plan

    We read your plan, the seat counts and the use of each zone, and agree what the room needs to do.

  2. 2

    Specification and tender documents

    Manufacturer advice, CAD/BIM files and an FF&E schedule you can put straight into the tender.

    CH-01TB-03
  3. 3

    Samples and finishes

    Fabric, wood and frame colours in your hands, reviewed with the client before anything is ordered.

  4. 4

    Ordering, logistics, delivery

    Orders placed with each manufacturer, shipments coordinated and delivered to the site in sequence.

  5. 5

    Installation and acceptance

    Furniture placed to the plan and checked against the schedule, then handed over.
